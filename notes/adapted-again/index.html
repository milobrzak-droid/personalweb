What changed me · Adapted

What covid took from me, and why we’re building Adapted differently

Something you can’t control can always come. But you can build so it doesn’t break you.

Milo Brzák · No playbook notes · November 2026

We founded Adapted when I first went to the Adaptive Surfing World Championship and there was no community in Czechia. Over time came the first Czech adaptive surf team, the first surf camp for people with disabilities in Bali and the Bali Adaptive Pro contest.

More than 120 people went through our camps. We supported several athletes and events. And we showed that disability and sport belong together. We talked about it in talks and in the media. I’m proud of that.

Then covid came. Within a short time, we lost our sponsors. At the same time, I lost my hotel in Bali.

Two things I take from it

First: an external shock you can’t control can always come. No plan rules it out. It’s just there. But you can build so it doesn’t break everything.

Second: don’t build things that rest on one person. Adapted rested on me. We had a great team, but when I couldn’t keep pulling, nobody was there to take over my role. That wasn’t the team’s mistake. It was a mistake in how I built it.

Now again, and differently

We’re rebuilding Adapted now. With more people who make decisions, more partners and more resources. So it works even when one of us drops out.

I ask the same question in companies today. What happens when the person everything rests on drops out? The answer is always the same. You have to build for it before it happens.

Have a thought on this? I’d be glad to hear a different view.
